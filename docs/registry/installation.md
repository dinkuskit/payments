1. Install and connect
   [DinkusKit Commerce](https://plugins.emdashcms.com/plugins/@smokyco.bsky.social/dinkus-commerce)
   first. Its connection creates the store that Payments joins.
2. Install DinkusKit Payments from the Registry in your EmDash admin.
3. Open **Dinkus Payments > Payment settings**, press **Connect**, and follow
   the link to approve at DinkusKit.com.
4. Follow the
   [Payments guide](https://dinkuskit.com/docs/reference/payments)
   through Stripe test setup and checking the connection.
5. Complete the documented test-order workflow and confirm the order in
   Commerce before considering live payments.

Your EmDash site needs Registry sandbox support. Installing a plugin does not
complete the merchant account's setup or enable live payments by itself.
